Partner program terms & privacy

Version 2026-11-12 · Referral entry requires Masso 3.0.1 or later. Availability of the new app version depends on Apple review and your region.

Attribution and offers

Codes identify a referral, not a guaranteed discount. Apple determines subscription eligibility, availability, price, trial and renewal terms. A customer must bind a valid code in Masso before the initial subscription; the first confirmed referral is retained. The referral window is 30 days. Old subscriptions, repeat purchases, self-referrals and fraudulent transactions are ineligible. We cannot verify whether a referred user follows your social account. Codes cannot be renamed or reassigned. Disabling stops new use; existing purchases remain valid.

Commission

The standard rate is 20% of reconciled developer proceeds on the first eligible paid transaction, excluding trial periods, taxes, Apple fees, refunds and adjustments. There is no lifetime renewal commission. Statements become eligible only after financial reconciliation and at least 30 days of observation. Eligible is not paid. Refunds can reverse pending commission or be offset against a future payment. We may suspend suspicious activity for review. Changes apply to future agreements, not prior earned transactions. A payment arrangement and applicable identity/tax requirements must be completed before payout; no automatic payout is promised.

Promotion

Promotions must accurately describe Masso and disclose the commercial relationship where required. Do not promise guaranteed fitness results, misrepresent pricing, send unsolicited bulk messages, impersonate Masso or misuse someone else's code.

Privacy

We store partner email/name, hashed login sessions, referral codes, anonymous app referral identifiers, verified subscription transactions and commission records to operate this program. Email sign-in uses our configured email provider. Link visits are deduplicated daily using a salted hash and retained for up to 90 days; they are not exact people or installs. The partner dashboard exposes only aggregated referrals and commission statements, never customer names, contact details, photos or health/training data. Private login sessions expire after seven days. Transaction and settlement records are retained as needed for reconciliation, disputes and applicable obligations. Contact Masso through the support link on its App Store page for privacy or program questions. See also the Masso privacy policy.
