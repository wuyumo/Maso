This browser demo redistributes a converted copy of Masso's approved training
model, Maso/Resources/Anatomy/MuscleBody.scn. Mesh names and the muscle-region
mapping match MuscleBodyMapping.json. The file was not redrawn.

Change notice: landmark and high-poly limb meshes were grid-decimated so a
phone browser can rotate them. Chest, abs, obliques, lats, back, glutes, and
leg meshes at or under 4,000 triangles are unchanged. Colors in the demo are
applied at runtime for this session's example training volume. They are not the
artist's original materials and are not a medical fatigue measurement.

This preview is not connected to a real workout or to Health data. It does not
represent the artist's endorsement.
