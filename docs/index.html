What the current listing describes

The points below are the App Store description checked on {{ site.listing_checked }}, plus the limits stated in the privacy policy and terms. This page does not add features that listing does not describe.

  • AI Coach. Tell it your goals, available time, and equipment. Create a plan, refine it in chat, and choose which preferences to remember. AI needs a network connection and your permission before personal content is sent.
  • Logging. Log weight and reps, use rest timers, and follow the session on Apple Watch or with Live Activities.
  • Recovery. See a training-based muscle recovery estimate, including the 3D muscle view named on the listing, and exercises for each muscle. That estimate is not a medical measurement and not medical advice.
  • Progress. Compare lifts, review muscle training volume, and revisit workout history. The listing says lift-progress analysis is part of Masso Pro.
  • Meals. Describe a meal or add a photo to estimate protein, carbohydrates, and fat against your targets. Those figures are estimates, not medical measurements.
  • Sleep. Connect Apple Health to see sleep duration, stages, and progress toward a sleep target you choose. Stages require compatible Apple Health data.
  • The day, and the Home Screen. Workout, Diet, and Sleep can sit on one calendar. The listing includes small, medium, and large Home Screen widgets.

Download price and Masso Pro

Masso is free to download. This website does not sell Masso Pro, does not show a checkout, and does not receive your card details.

On the United States App Store listing checked on {{ site.listing_checked }}:

  • Basic workout logging and history stay free.
  • You can save up to five workout plans.
  • Free AI requests continue after you close the upgrade reminder. That sentence does not mean every AI feature is free or unlimited.
  • Masso Pro Monthly was listed at US$4.99 in the United States, HK$38 in Hong Kong, £4.99 in the United Kingdom, and S$6.98 in Singapore.
  • Masso Pro Yearly was listed at US$29.99, HK$228, £29.99, and S$39.98 on those same pages.
  • Masso Pro adds unlimited saved plans, AI without upgrade reminders, lift-progress analysis, detailed muscle recovery, and custom exercises.
  • Eligible subscribers can receive a 7-day free trial when Apple offers one.
  • Subscriptions renew until you cancel them. No new lifetime plan is offered.

Apple shows eligibility, price, and billing period for your storefront before you purchase. If this page and the store disagree, pay the price Apple shows.
