# Masso

A fitness tracking app for people who actually train.

- [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html)
- [Terms of Service](./terms.html)

---

**Masso** is an offline-first iOS workout tracker. No Masso account is required. Core records stay on your device. Optional AI processes the content you choose to share with your permission. Purchases, AI, feedback and uncached exercise media need internet access. Optional usage analytics is off until you enable it. Read our [privacy policy](./privacy-policy.html) for details.
