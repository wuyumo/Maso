Where do I download Masso?

Download Masso — AI Workout Planner on the App Store. The developer and seller on that listing is Yumo Wu. This website does not host the app file and does not take payment.

Do I need an account?

No Masso account is required. The privacy policy says workout plans, sets, progress, meal records, selected photos, sleep summaries, and preferences are stored on your device. An iOS backup may keep a separate copy, depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local data and does not erase that backup.

Local storage does not mean every feature works offline. The privacy policy says AI, purchases, feedback, and exercise media that is not already cached need a network connection.

How does AI Coach work?

The App Store description says you can tell Masso your goals, available time, and equipment, create a plan, refine it in chat, and choose which preferences to remember. AI features need internet access and your permission to share relevant data.

In version 3.0 and later, the privacy policy says Masso asks before sending personal content for AI processing. You can decline and keep using basic workout logging, history, and local calculations. You can withdraw permission in Settings → AI data sharing. What a request may contain, and which providers receive it, is listed in the privacy policy, not restated here.

Is recovery, nutrition, or sleep medical advice?

No. The App Store description and the terms say meal nutrients and muscle recovery are estimates, not medical measurements or medical advice. Sleep stages require compatible Apple Health data. Masso does not diagnose, treat, or prevent a disease or condition. Read the terms before you train.

What stays free, and what is Masso Pro?

The app is free to download. On the United States listing checked on {{ site.listing_checked }}, basic workout logging and history stay free. That listing says you can save up to five workout plans and continue free AI requests after closing the upgrade reminder.

The same listing says Masso Pro adds unlimited saved plans, AI without upgrade reminders, lift-progress analysis, detailed muscle recovery, and custom exercises. It showed two optional auto-renewing subscriptions: Masso Pro Monthly at US$4.99 and Masso Pro Yearly at US$29.99. Eligible subscribers can receive a 7-day free trial when Apple offers one. Apple shows eligibility, price, and billing period before purchase. Other countries, and later price changes, can differ. No new lifetime plan is offered. This website does not sell either subscription.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

Apple processes new subscriptions. Cancel in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Masso. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Restoration uses the Apple ID that bought it. If you bought Pro earlier through Polar, the privacy policy and terms describe that existing licence. This site cannot look up your purchase.

Does Masso use ads or analytics?

The privacy policy says Masso does not sell personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other companies’ apps. It does not say the app is analytics-free.

Optional usage analytics stays off in version 3.0 and later until you explicitly enable “Share anonymous usage data” in Settings. Earlier versions may have enabled it by default; the privacy policy says you can turn it off in Settings. The policy names the provider and the kinds of events. This page does not add a different analytics description.

Does Masso work with Apple Watch and Apple Health?

Health permissions are optional and separate. The privacy policy says Masso can read authorized sleep stages and workout summaries, and can write completed workouts. The Watch app can record workouts, heart rate, and energy in Apple Health. The App Store description also says you can follow a session with Live Activities. Sleep stages need compatible Apple Health data. Turning a permission off is done in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Local records stay until you edit or delete them, or until you delete the app. An Apple backup may still hold a copy. Feedback you chose to send, optional analytics already sent, and purchase records held by Apple or Polar are described in the privacy policy.
