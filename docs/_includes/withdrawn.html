Withdrawn statements

An earlier version of this website said “iOS App Store: coming soon” and “No accounts, no ads, no analytics — your training data stays on your device.” Those sentences are withdrawn.

Masso is available on the App Store. It still does not require a Masso account, and the privacy policy says it does not use advertising SDKs or sell personal data. It does have optional usage analytics. In version 3.0 and later that analytics stays off until you turn on “Share anonymous usage data” in Settings. Earlier versions may have enabled it by default. The privacy policy is the description of what the app does with data.
